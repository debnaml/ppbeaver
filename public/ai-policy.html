AI Policy

Last updated: April 2026

This page sets out how Performance Peak (PP Worldwide Ltd, UK Companies House 15037470) wishes its content to be used by AI systems. It applies to all pages on performancepeak.co.uk.

Our position in one paragraph

We welcome retrieval and citation by user-driven AI assistants — where a person asks a question and the assistant fetches and cites our pages. We do not consent to our content being collected, copied or used to train AI models, foundation models, or commercial datasets. Both stances are signalled in our robots.txt and via response headers.

Indexing

Conventional search engine indexing is allowed. Our pages are intended to be discoverable.

Retrieval (allowed)

The following user-driven retrieval bots are explicitly allowed:

  • OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User
  • PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User
  • Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User
  • Google-CloudVertexBot

When citing our content, please link back to the source page and attribute it to Performance Peak.

Training (not allowed)

The following bots associated with training and dataset collection are disallowed:

  • GPTBot
  • ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai
  • Google-Extended
  • CCBot (Common Crawl)
  • Bytespider, Amazonbot, FacebookBot, meta-externalagent
  • Applebot-Extended, cohere-ai, Diffbot, Omgilibot, PetalBot, Timpibot, YouBot, ImagesiftBot

Use of any content from this site as training data, or for the creation of derivative datasets, is not permitted without prior written consent.

Text and Data Mining (TDM) reservation

In line with Article 4(3) of EU Directive 2019/790 and equivalent UK provisions, Performance Peak expressly reserves its rights against text and data mining of any content on this domain for the purpose of training AI models or producing commercial datasets. This reservation is also signalled by the X-Robots-Tag: noai, noimageai response header set on all pages.

Generated content

Do not present AI-generated text or images as having been authored or endorsed by Performance Peak.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests for licensed access to content for research purposes, should be sent to hello@performancepeak.co.uk.
